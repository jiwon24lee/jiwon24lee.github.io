    <p>
      Outside of research, I enjoy playing the electric guitar and listening to guitar-driven music.
      I often rehearse and play cover songs with old bandmates from university, and we've had some great times practicing together in rehearsal studios.
      When I'm alone, I usually pick a song to practice on my own.
      Being a big fan of rock and heavy metal, my choices tend to be technically demanding and often take months to master.
      But when I finally get through them, it's always a very rewarding experience.
      On top of that, I've also been exploring a broader range of music—from Korean indie bands like <em>Decadent</em> and <em>Life and Time</em> to genres such as fusion and blues.
      Currently, I'm working on <em>The Lights Behind You</em> by <em>SURL</em>.
      This variety helps me stay inspired and continuously expand my musical vocabulary.
    </p>
