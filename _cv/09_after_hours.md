    <p>
      Outside of research, I enjoy playing electric guitar and listening to guitar-driven music.
      I often rehearse and play cover songs with my former university bandmates, and we’ve had many great sessions together in rehearsal studios.
      When I’m practicing solo, I usually pick a song and work through it on my own.
      As a big fan of rock and heavy metal, I tend to gravitate toward technically demanding pieces that can take months to master.
      But finally nailing them is always incredibly rewarding.
      Lately, I’ve also been exploring a broader range of music—from Korean indie bands like <em>Decadent</em> and <em>Life and Time</em> to genres such as fusion and blues.
      These days, I’m working on <em>The Lights Behind You</em> by <em>SURL</em>.
      Keeping this mix of styles helps me stay inspired and continuously expand my musical vocabulary.
    </p>
